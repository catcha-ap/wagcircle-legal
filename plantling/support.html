A few helpful notes

Frequently asked questions

How do I contact Plantling?

Send a note to catcha.asap@gmail.com. Include the app version shown in Settings if you’re reporting an issue.

Can I ask about a plant problem?

Yes. Plantling includes a plant diagnosis flow and Lily Chat for plant-care questions. Their suggestions are informational and can be incomplete; check your plant’s condition and consult reliable sources when needed.

Where are my photos and plant notes?

Plantling keeps some app information on your device and stores submitted scan photos and supported records in private cloud storage associated with your app installation. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How can I remove my Plantling data?

Open Settings → Delete all data in the app. This removes supported Plantling records and associated cloud photos. It does not currently delete the anonymous authentication account or immediately clear provider backups and logs.

My reminder didn’t appear. What should I check?

Check that notifications are allowed for Plantling in iOS Settings and that the reminder is enabled in the app. You can still review your plant reminders in My Plants.

Can I send a photo by email?

You can, but avoid including people, addresses, or other personal details in the image. If your question is about a scan already in Plantling, describe the issue first and we’ll let you know what information would help.
