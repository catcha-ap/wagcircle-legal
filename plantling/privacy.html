Privacy Policy

A clear look at what Plantling handles, why it’s needed, and the choices you have.

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Plantling (“Plantling,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use the Plantling iOS app and this website. Plantling is operated by the app developer. This policy describes the app’s current features and may change as they do.

Plantling uses an anonymous app account to associate your plants and app activity with your installation. You do not currently create a password-based account in the app.

Information we handle

  • Plant and care information: plant names, species, notes, care logs, reminders, and related settings you add.
  • Photos and scan records: photos you submit for plant identification or diagnosis, plus records needed to show your scan history. Photos are stored in Plantling’s private cloud storage under your anonymous app account.
  • Chat content: messages and optional photos you choose to send in Lily Chat, along with conversation records used to show conversation history.
  • Device and notification information: an app-generated anonymous account identifier and, if you enable push notifications, a device push token and app version used to deliver plant care reminders.
  • On-device information: app preferences, cached images, and drafts may be stored locally on your device.

Please avoid including personal or sensitive information in plant names, notes, photos, or messages. Photos may incidentally contain people or other details in the background.

How it is used

We use this information to provide app features you request: saving your plants and care history, syncing supported records, displaying scan history, responding to plant questions, producing identification or diagnosis results, and delivering reminders you enable. We also use it to maintain, protect, and troubleshoot the app.

Plant photos and relevant prompts are sent to the services needed to provide AI-assisted plant identification, diagnosis, and chat. For a chat response, the question and relevant conversation context are processed to generate an answer. AI results can be incomplete or inaccurate; see our Terms.

The app does not currently ask you to create a profile with a name or password. If you email support, we receive the email address and message details you choose to send.

Website visits

This is a static informational website. It does not offer an account, newsletter, or contact form. If you visit it, the website hosting provider may receive standard connection information (such as your IP address, browser, requested page, and request time) in order to serve and protect the site. The hosting provider’s handling and retention of server logs depends on the hosting configuration. The pages use system fonts and do not load third-party font services.

Service providers

Plantling uses Supabase for anonymous authentication, database and private photo storage, and server functions. When you use AI features, the relevant photo, prompt, or conversation context is processed by our AI service provider (OpenAI) to generate a response. Push notification delivery uses Apple Push Notification service (APNs). These providers process information to support the requested features and under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not sell your personal information.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to respond to valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the service.

Storage & security

Cloud records and submitted photos are associated with your anonymous app account and protected by access controls intended to limit access to that account. Data travels through network services to provide cloud features. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Information may be processed in locations where Plantling or its service providers operate. When you use AI features, the content needed to answer your request is transmitted to the AI provider. Do not send anything through Plantling that you are not comfortable having processed to provide that feature.

Retention & deletion

We keep cloud records and photos while needed to provide the app and its history, unless you delete them sooner or we need to retain them for a legitimate operational or legal reason. You can use Settings → Delete all data in the app to request deletion of your Plantling cloud records and associated stored photos, and to clear supported local app data. The app may retain the anonymous authentication session so it can continue to work; this action is not the same as deleting the authentication account. Service logs and backups may persist for limited periods under provider retention practices.

For help with a deletion request, contact catcha.asap@gmail.com.

Your choices

Before using AI features, Plantling asks for explicit permission to send relevant messages, photos, conversation history or summaries, selected plant details and care history, language, time zone, and region to OpenAI for responses and content safety checks. Manage or withdraw permission in Settings → Privacy → AI assistance. Withdrawal stops future AI requests and does not delete prior records. OpenAI API data is not used for training by default; provider safety logs may generally be retained for up to 30 days, with exceptions and other provider-specific retention. AI permission choices are stored under your anonymous app account.

You can report an AI reply from the chat. The reply and selected reason are available to Plantling support for review. Reports are removed with the conversation or the delete-all-data action. We may pause AI access for abuse. We remain responsible for ensuring service providers protect information consistently with this policy.

You choose whether to provide photos or chat messages, and can manage notification permission through iOS Settings. You can delete plant records or use the app’s delete-all-data action. Some app features need cloud processing to work.

You may also stop using the app and remove it from your device. Removing the app clears its on-device files according to iOS behavior, but does not by itself send a request to delete data already stored in the cloud. Use Settings → Delete all data before uninstalling if you want Plantling to delete the supported cloud records and photos associated with that installation.

Children’s privacy

Plantling is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will update the effective date above and provide additional notice in the app or on this page when appropriate.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email catcha.asap@gmail.com.
