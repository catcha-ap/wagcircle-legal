Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Your privacy matters to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how GoalPop handles information when you use the app. GoalPop is designed to keep the goal-setting experience simple and, where possible, keep your personal goal information on your device.

1. Information We Collect

GoalPop does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal account information to use the core features of the app.

2. Goals, Tasks, Progress, and Rewards

Information you create in GoalPop, such as goals, tasks, progress, completion history, and reward details, is stored locally on your device.

GoalPop does not upload this information to our servers. The current app has no backend or cloud-sync service.

3. Photos and Images

GoalPop may request access to your photo library when you choose to add a photo to a reward. The app accesses only the image you select for this purpose.

Selected images and reward sticker assets are processed and saved locally in the app's storage. We found no code that uploads them to GoalPop servers. You can control photo access through the privacy settings on your device.

4. Feedback and Email

If you choose to contact us through the Send Feedback feature or by email, we receive the information you voluntarily include in your message, such as your email address, feedback, screenshots, or troubleshooting information.

We use this information only to respond to your request, improve GoalPop, or investigate technical issues.

Contact: catcha.asap@gmail.com

5. In-App Purchases

GoalPop offers an optional one-time consumable in-app purchase called “Buy Me a Coffee.” The purchase is processed through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. GoalPop does not receive or store your payment card number, Apple Account password, or other payment credentials.

GoalPop receives limited transaction information from Apple, such as the product identifier, purchase result, and transaction verification information, so the app can complete and verify the purchase. We do not use this information for advertising or behavioral profiling.

The purchase does not unlock features, create an account, or change how GoalPop works. Apple may process your purchase information under its own privacy policy and terms.

6. Analytics and Tracking

GoalPop does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites for advertising purposes.

GoalPop does not currently use third-party analytics services to collect behavioral analytics.

7. Advertising

GoalPop does not display third-party advertisements.

8. Children's Privacy

GoalPop is a general-purpose goal and reward app and is not intended to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the core GoalPop experience.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request or other communication, please contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete that information.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share your GoalPop goal data with advertisers or data brokers.

10. Data Retention

Goal information stored locally remains on your device until you delete the information, remove the app, or otherwise erase the application's local data.

Information you voluntarily send to us may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain support records, or address legal and security obligations.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by GoalPop. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as GoalPop evolves.

When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or GoalPop's privacy practices, contact us at:
