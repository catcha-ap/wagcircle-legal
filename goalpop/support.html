Common Help Topics

How is my goal data stored?

Your goals, tasks, progress, completion history, and reward information are stored locally on your device. The current app does not use cloud storage or a backend.

How do I send feedback?

Open GoalPop Settings and select “Send Feedback,” or email us directly at catcha.asap@gmail.com.

Why can't GoalPop send an email from my device?

GoalPop uses your device's available email apps to start a support message. If no compatible email service is available, you can contact us directly at catcha.asap@gmail.com.

Can I recover deleted goals?

Currently, deleted GoalPop goals cannot be restored. Please make sure you want to remove a goal before confirming deletion.
