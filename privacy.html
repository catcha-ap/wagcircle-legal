Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how WagCircle collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the WagCircle dog-walking app. Please review this product-ready draft and replace the placeholders after confirming the production data flows.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, WagCircle may collect:

  • Account information, including email and authentication data.
  • Owner profile information and dog profile information such as name, breed, birthday, weight, sex, and photos.
  • Walk information, including start and end times, duration, distance, GPS route points, and completion, pause, cancellation, or interruption status.
  • Pee, poop, and water events, including their times and any details you choose to provide.
  • Technical, diagnostic, performance, and crash information needed to operate and improve the app.
  • Analytics data only if an analytics feature is enabled in the production app.

How We Use Information

We use information to manage accounts and dog profiles; record walks and calculate distance and duration; display routes; provide History, Insights, daily goals, and streaks; provide weather information and walking suggestions; respond to support requests; maintain security; improve reliability; and process purchases if purchases are enabled.

Location Information

WagCircle uses location during an active walk to record route points, distance, and movement. The current app may require Always location permission so an active walk can continue to record when the app is in the background. You control location permissions through iOS Settings. Location behavior and permission wording must be confirmed against the production build before publication.

Photos and Pet Information

Owner and dog photos are optional uploads used to display your profile and dog profiles in the app. WagCircle does not claim to analyze photos unless that feature is separately implemented and disclosed.

Data Storage and Service Providers

WagCircle currently uses Supabase for authentication, database services, and pet-photo storage. It uses Apple Sign in only if enabled for your account, Apple MapKit and Core Location for maps and location features, and Apple WeatherKit with an Open-Meteo fallback for weather features. We do not describe Mixpanel, RevenueCat, Superwall, or other providers here unless they are enabled in the production app.

Analytics and App Improvements

If enabled, analytics may describe feature usage, performance, errors, crashes, and adoption. WagCircle does not state that precise GPS routes are sent to analytics unless that data flow is actually implemented and disclosed.

Data Retention

Account and dog data are retained while your account is active. Walk history may be retained to provide History and Insights. Deletion requests are handled through the account-deletion flow, subject to limited legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting exceptions. Confirm exact retention periods before publication.

Account Deletion

You can request account deletion through the in-app account-deletion flow, if available, or by contacting support. The production deletion flow is intended to address your profile, dog profiles, photos, walks, routes, logs, and daily goals; confirm the actual cascade behavior before publication.

Your Privacy Choices

You may update account information, manage iOS permissions, remove photos, request account deletion, and contact support about your information.

Children’s Privacy

WagCircle is not directed to children. Do not use the service if you are not permitted to do so under the applicable age requirement.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as the app or our practices change. We will update the “Last updated” date when changes are made.
